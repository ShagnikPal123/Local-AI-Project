How it works

1Get your access keyThe owner sends you a key that starts with NYX1-. Redeem it in Nyx (Settings → Access) — it's also how this site knows it's you.
2Send from Nyx, or hereIn Nyx, open the Collab tab, tick what you changed and press Send. Or use the form below with your files.
3Reviewed on GitHubEach change lands on its own beta/… branch as a pull request. Nothing is merged until the owner approves it.

Send a change

Easiest from Nyx's Collab tab. Here you can send files or feedback from any browser.

Drop text files here, or choose files. Set each file's path in the project.

Up to 30 files, 300 KB each. Personal data (chats, memory, keys, uploads) and anything that looks like a secret is refused.

The key is sent only to check who you are and to send your change. This site never stores it; "Remember" keeps it in this browser only.
