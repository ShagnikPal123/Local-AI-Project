# Online Results Training Log

This file is reserved for later use when Nyx Pulse is allowed to ingest, review, and learn from online model results.

## Purpose

Capture structured examples that could eventually be used to improve prompts, behavior, or quality without directly mixing all online traffic into the live app.

## Rules

- Only log reviewed, useful results.
- Keep examples small and human-readable.
- Include source, date, prompt context, and outcome.
- Separate raw outputs from curated training examples.
- Do not use this log for unreviewed or sensitive data.

## Log Format

- Date:
- Source provider:
- Prompt summary:
- Model output summary:
- Why it was useful:
- Quality notes:
- Approved for training?:
- Tags:

## Example

- Why it was useful: concise and actionable
- Quality notes: strong structure, moderate verbosity
